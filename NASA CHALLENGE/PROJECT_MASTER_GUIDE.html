1. Executive Summary & Elevator Pitch

30-Second Elevator Pitch

"NASA's Mars Trek lets you look at Mars from orbit—but it doesn't tell an astronaut walking on the surface whether they will survive the walk. Mars Pathfinder is an interactive EVA survival and route-planning system for Jezero Crater. It combines 7 real NASA orbital and rover datasets with an obstacle-avoiding A* pathfinder and a real NASA xEMU spacesuit life-support simulator. Instead of drawing a straight line over a deadly cliff, Mars Pathfinder plots the safest walkable route, tells the astronaut exactly how much oxygen, battery, and radiation dose it will cost, and automatically reroutes them back to shelter if a Solar Storm hits."

2-Minute Presentation Pitch

"When humans land in Jezero Crater, they won't just need a pretty map—they'll need a real-time tactical survival computer. Existing planetary portals like Mars Trek are built for orbital sightseeing, not for an astronaut wearing a pressurized spacesuit in 38% Earth gravity, -62°C cold, and 748 Pa near-vacuum.

Our project, Mars Pathfinder (ARES-V), connects a 360° ball-roll 3D Mars Globe with 2D Jezero EVA Mission Control overlaid with 7 NASA datasets: HiRISE cliff hazards (>15°–20°), THEMIS sand traps, CRISM biosignature minerals, MEDA cryogenic cold shadows (-114.2°C), Perseverance's actual rover path, science targets, and 10m elevation contours.

An astronaut can start from Base Camp or drop anywhere on Jezero Crater, plot a hazard-safe A* route, verify exact walk time (0.80 m/s Mars pace) and xEMU PLSS oxygen consumption (530 L / 3,000 psi tank, 20.1 kJ/L metabolic conversion), and trigger an automated emergency return if a +48 mSv/hr Solar Particle Event strikes."

2. Plain-English Walkthrough of Every Screen Feature

  • 3D Mars Globe vs. 2D Jezero EVA Map: Drag the 3D Mars Globe infinitely in any direction (Quaternion Ball-Roll with zero pole lock) to inspect global landmarks, then zoom into Jezero Crater or click Jezero EVA (2D) for 25 cm/px HiRISE surface routing across 6 key sites: Octavia E. Butler Landing (Base Camp), Western Delta Front (Kodiak Butte), Neretva Vallis Inflow Channel, Carbonate Margin, Séítah Dune & Boulder Field, and Belva Impact Crater Rim.
  • The 7 Layered NASA Datasets (Left Sidebar): Toggleable pill switches with opacity sliders for Red (HiRISE slope/cliff hazards), Orange (THEMIS sand traps), Teal (CRISM minerals/biosignatures), Purple (MEDA cold shadows & RAD radiation), Gold (Perseverance Sol 0–1324 ground-truth traverse), Cyan (Base Camp & Science Targets), and Blue (10m Topographic Contours).
  • Smart A* Routing & Flexible Origin: Uses a 65×65 hazard-aware grid over Jezero Crater to avoid cliffs and sand dunes. Use Click: Target / Click: Origin or Swap to plan from any drop-off point or back to Base Camp, and tune penalties live via the ⚙ button.
  • Spacesuit Life-Support (xEMU PLSS) & Solar Storm (SPE): Circular gauges display Post-Trip O₂ (%) and Suit Battery (%). Adjust Current PLSS O₂ Reserve (10%–100%) to test Go/No-Go feasibility, or click Solar Storm (SPE) to trigger automated emergency return routing to Base Camp airlock.

6. Anticipated Judge Q&A Cheat Sheet

1. Why can't astronauts just use NASA's Mars Trek?
Mars Trek is for orbital viewing; Mars Pathfinder adds hazard-aware A* routing around cliffs and sand traps plus real xEMU PLSS oxygen, battery, and radiation calculations.
2. Are the oxygen and walk-time numbers real?
Yes: 0.80 m/s (2.88 km/h) Mars pressurized pace from NASA EVA ConOps, 293 W nominal load at 20.1 kJ/L O₂ from NASA-STD-3001, and 530 L / 3,000 psi from NASA/TM-20205008709.
3. What if an astronaut is already low on oxygen?
The Current PLSS O₂ Reserve slider tests any starting tank level and flashes ABORT: O₂ INSUFFICIENT if the route exceeds remaining O₂.
4. Why have both a 3D Globe and a 2D Map?
The 3D Mars Globe gives planetary context (rollable 360° in any direction), while zooming into Jezero enters 25 cm/px 2D tactical pathfinding.
5. Does the astronaut always start at Base Camp?
No—use Click: Origin or Swap to route from any rover drop-off or mid-EVA point on Jezero Crater.
6. What does Solar Storm (SPE) do?
Simulates a +48 mSv/hr proton spike, triggers an alarm, aborts sampling, and plots the fastest safe A* return route to Base Camp airlock.
7. How does it handle steep cliffs and loose sand?
Cliffs >15°–20° are impassable in the A* grid and THEMIS sand dunes carry a 5.0x penalty (all tunable live via ⚙).
8. Does it require a backend server?
No—it is 100% client-side HTML5/JS/CSS and deploys directly on GitHub Pages.
